# Keyflow typing practice

A dependency-free typing tutor that runs in a browser. Open `index.html` to practice; no server or account is required.

Choose a difficulty, passage topic, and a 1-, 2-, 3-, or 5-minute test. When you finish a passage, the next one loads without resetting the timer. **Next passage** skips ahead while keeping the current test going; **Restart** starts over with the selected settings. The live stats show words per minute and accuracy, and the results include a style score based on accuracy (50%), keystroke rhythm (30%), and pace (20%). Your best score is saved in browser storage when available.

The score is a practice aid, not a medical or ergonomic assessment. The lessons offer general technique reminders, but the app cannot observe posture or finger placement.
